# Hypothesis Engine & Lifecycle

## Overview
Diagnostic explanations in NEXUS are treated as scientific hypotheses subject to verification, partial support, or rejection against empirical data.

---

## Hypothesis Structure
```json
{
  "id": "INV-A1B2C3D4-HYP-CAT",
  "statement": "The revenue decline was concentrated within specific merchandise categories.",
  "type": "category_contribution",
  "supporting_evidence": [
    {
      "tool_name": "run_variance_analysis",
      "metric": "category_contribution_share",
      "source": "sales_and_products",
      "value": "Electronics: 62.4%",
      "contribution_pct": 62.4,
      "notes": "Top category contributor 'Electronics' accounted for 62.4% of total measured variance (-$5,100.00)."
    }
  ],
  "contradicting_evidence": [],
  "evidence_strength": "DIRECT",
  "status": "SUPPORTED",
  "confidence_reason": "Direct attribution: 'Electronics' accounted for 62.4% of measured revenue variance.",
  "limitations": []
}
```

---

## Hypothesis Statuses
- **SUPPORTED**: Empirical data directly corroborates the candidate explanation with material contribution.
- **PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED**: The factor contributed alongside other concurrent drivers.
- **NOT_SUPPORTED**: Data rules out the hypothesis (e.g. mix effect was negligible or variance was diffuse).
- **INCONCLUSIVE**: Insufficient data exists to evaluate the assertion.

---

## No Fabricated Confidence Scores
NEXUS rejects arbitrary percentage probabilities generated by LLMs (e.g., "87% confidence"). Instead, confidence is grounded in the **Evidence Strength Hierarchy** (`DIRECT`, `STRONG`, `MODERATE`, `WEAK`, `INSUFFICIENT`).
