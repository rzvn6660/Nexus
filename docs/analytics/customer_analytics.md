# Customer Analytics, RFM & Cohort Analysis

Deterministic behavioral analytics model how customers purchase, repeat, and retain over time.

---

## 1. RFM Segmentation

RFM evaluates three primary customer behavioral dimensions:
1. **Recency ($R$)**: Elapsed calendar days between the evaluation date and the customer's most recent completed order.
2. **Frequency ($F$)**: Total distinct completed orders fulfilled within the evaluation scope.
3. **Monetary ($M$)**: Total net commercial revenue generated by the customer.

### Scoring Methodology
- Customers are sorted and ranked into $N$ equal quantiles (default: 5 quintiles, 1–5).
- **Recency Inversion**: Lower elapsed days represents higher engagement; hence, lowest recency days receive score 5, and highest recency receives score 1.
- **Frequency & Monetary**: Higher values receive higher scores (score 5).
- **Segment Assignment**: Deterministic rules map 3-digit RFM codes (e.g. `555`, `111`) to intuitive business segments (*Champions*, *Loyal Customers*, *At Risk*, *Hibernating*, *Potential Loyalists*).

---

## 2. Cohort Retention Analysis

Customers are grouped into monthly cohorts based on their initial `Customer.acquisition_date` (format: `YYYY-MM`).

For each subsequent monthly offset $M+k$ (where $k \ge 0$ represents months elapsed since acquisition):
- **Active Customers**: Count of unique customers from the cohort who completed at least one transaction in month offset $k$.
- **Retention Rate**: $(\text{Active Customers} / \text{Initial Cohort Size}) \times 100\%$.
- **Total Spend**: Net commercial revenue contributed by the cohort in month offset $k$.
- **Average Spend per Active**: $\text{Total Spend} / \text{Active Customers}$.

---

## 3. Repeat Purchase Dynamics

Measures customer order concentration and loyalty:
- **One-time Customers**: Number of distinct buyers who placed exactly 1 order.
- **Repeat Customers**: Number of distinct buyers who placed $\ge 2$ orders.
- **Repeat Purchase Rate**: $(\text{Repeat Customers} / \text{Total Ordering Customers}) \times 100\%$.
- **Frequency Distribution**: Bins customer order counts into `1`, `2`, `3-5`, and `6+` orders.
